# Code Plants

Code Plants connects coding activity with caring for a personal plant shelf.
These terms describe the agreed plant-care model; they do not imply that it is implemented yet.

## Language

**Plant food**:
A reward earned through active editing, with writing and deleting both contributing. Food belongs to a shared shelf balance and is spent manually on a chosen plant.
_Avoid_: Points, coins, XP

**Health**:
A plant’s temporary care condition: resting, content, or thriving. Inactivity can leave a plant resting but never causes sickness, death, or loss of growth.
_Avoid_: Survival meter

**Growth**:
A plant’s permanent development. Previously earned growth and upgrades are retained during inactivity.
_Avoid_: Health

**Active editing**:
Time spent making changes, including writing and deleting, rather than the volume of characters or lines changed. Writing, deleting, pasting, and undoing in workspace text files all qualify; reading and idle time do not.
_Avoid_: Lines written

**Feeding**:
Spending plant food on a chosen plant to improve its health and add permanent growth progress. A thriving plant can still be fed for growth.
_Avoid_: Watering

**Garden**:
The plants and shared food balance belonging to one workspace. Separate workspaces have separate gardens.
_Avoid_: Global collection

**Growth stage**:
A plant’s permanent development milestone: Seedling, Growing, or Mature.
_Avoid_: Health condition

**Qualifying minute**:
A clock minute containing at least one qualifying workspace edit. It is a unit of activity credit, not a measurement of continuous typing time.
_Avoid_: Typing minute

**Food balance**:
The garden’s earned, unspent plant food, shared by all its plants. Earned food does not expire.
_Avoid_: Plant balance

**Growth point**:
Permanent progress granted by a feeding toward the plant’s next growth stage. Progress stops at maturity.
_Avoid_: Health point
